Organic Input Estimation Calculator

Calculate estimated organic manure, compost, or biofertiliser quantity needed based on your crop-specific application rate.

Input Parameters

Enter recommendation from your soil test card or crop package of practices.
How the Calculator Works
Estimated Quantity = Cultivated Area × Application Rate

This calculator does not prescribe universal fertiliser or manure application rates. Application rates differ substantially based on:

  • Crop Nutrient Demand: Heavy feeders (Paddy, Sugarcane) require substantially higher organic carbon inputs than pulses (Groundnut, Red gram).
  • Baseline Soil Organic Carbon: Soils testing below 0.5% SOC require supplementary basal compost compared to well-managed fertile tracts.
  • Moisture & Rain Regime: Irrigated plots can utilize larger quantities of bulky manure efficiently without scorch risks.
Important Guidance: Always obtain your recommended dose per acre from your soil test health card or local Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) advisory.
Common Application Rate Units Reference
1 Hectare= 2.471 Acres
1 Acre= 40 Gunthas = 100 Cents
1 Tonne= 1,000 Kilograms = 10 Quintals
1 Quintal= 100 Kilograms
